# HOMELANCER — Room Art Style Guide (LOCKED, owner, Oct 27 2026)

Every station room picture for every faction follows this. Do not drift from it.

## The style

- **Epic Seven anime style.** Clean cel-shaded anime characters with crisp line art, expressive faces, detailed
  costumes, like the Epic Seven character art. Not soft painterly, not semi-real, not photoreal.
- **Futuristic Homelancer world,** Star Citizen scale and detail: hard-surface sci-fi interiors, real machinery,
  real hangars and bridges.
- **Military space RPG / war game.** This is not a travel centre, airport or hotel. No luggage, no rolling suitcases,
  no tourists, no families with kids, no snack lines, no shopping-mall look.
- **Everyone is a pilot, a captain of their own ship, a fighter, a mercenary on a job, trying to survive.**
  People wear flight suits, armour, combat gear, holsters, sidearms, rifles on their backs, helmets under the arm,
  datapads with mission maps. Mixed races (humans, blue-skinned aliens, elf-eared, beast-folk), all battle-ready.
- **Weapons visible** in every room: sidearms, rifles, armour, weapon racks, ammo, ship guns.
- **Workers do their jobs:** mechanics, medics, officers, quartermasters, guards. Busy, purposeful, not idle.
- **The faction's own colours and emblem** on walls, banners and uniforms (see the faction list below).

## Picture layout (same for every room)

One 16:9 image, about 1672 x 941, split into two halves with a thin white horizontal line across the middle:
TOP = the FRONT view of the room (wide, eye level, looking straight in).
BOTTOM = the BACK view (same spot, turned 180 degrees).
Same eye height, lens, lighting and scale in both halves. Keep signs, posters and faces away from the left and right
edges of each half (those edges are joined into a 360 loop).

## The 10 rooms per faction

1. Main hub (arrival concourse: pilots coming off ships, armed, mission boards, security, ships outside)
2. Shipyard (fighters and gunships on pads, mechanics, loadout carts, missiles)
3. Mech dealer (combat mechs on gantries, mech blueprints on screens, pilots inspecting)
4. Weapons (armory: rifles, ship cannons, missiles, armour on racks, guards)
5. Supplies (ammo crates, shield cells, repair kits, fuel canisters; quartermaster counter)
6. Mission center (big holographic war map, contracts, bounty boards, squads planning)
7. Medical bay (wounded pilots in armour, medics, cybernetic repair, trauma beds)
8. Command (bridge / war room: officers, tactical hologram table, fleet on the big window)
9. Private quarters (a pilot's own cabin: bed, armour on a stand, weapon locker, desk with mission data, ship outside)
10. Pilots' lounge (a rough pilots' bar: armed mercenaries drinking, cards and dice, bounty screens, war stories)
