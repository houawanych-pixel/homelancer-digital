# Flight HUD (owner, Oct 27 2026) — mockup: flight_hud_mockup.png

- Bottom centre: radar; SPEED above it; COCKPIT (view) button next to the radar, GO TO on the other side; MAP opens
  from the radar too.
- Health right of the radar: SHIELD, LEFT WING, HULL, RIGHT WING, ENERGY bars with percentages (the hull in three
  sections = left wing, core, right wing).
- DOCK prompt at the very top centre, with the distance.
- No yellow hint text across the top.
- CONTACTS (left) and SHIP SYSTEMS (right) panels, scrolling, minimisable, colour coded (red hostile, white/blue other).
- Weapons and abilities are not all on screen while cruising: a WEAPONS button pops the fighting buttons up when you
  engage, and they fold away again (so a call can come in without weapon buttons in the way).
- COMMS button bottom right, above the aim stick.
