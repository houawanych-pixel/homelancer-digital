# Hub: one floating digital panel (owner spec, Oct 27 2026)

Homelancer uses ONE floating digital panel on the right side of the screen. It works like a personal tablet nobody is
holding. It stays fixed while the player looks round the panorama; its translucent background keeps the station,
characters, ship, mech and maintenance activity visible. One panel that changes pages: ship cards, weapon lists,
ability menus and calls never cover the screen at the same time.

## Navigation
Compact top navigation, five categories: Hub, Lounge, Hangar, Supplies, Pilot. Selecting one shows only its options.
Hangar -> Ships, Mechs, Weapons. Supplies -> cargo, medical supplies, other consumables. No row of ten room buttons.
Top-left title: the faction and the station (e.g. "UNITY · ORBITAL STATION"), not "Homelancer".

## Ship and mech cards
Name, level, image, equipment mounts, ability slots, of the vehicle the player actually owns.
- Tap the vehicle image to enlarge it inside the panel.
- Tap a weapon mount or ability slot to select it; highlight it, show its name, show what is installed or "empty".
Mechs switches the environment to the mech hangar and loads the mech card in the same panel (same pattern).

## Buying and equipping
1. Open the ship / mech card. 2. Zoom if needed, select a mount. 3. The panel becomes a scrolling list of compatible
equipment. 4. Each item: small image, name, price, brief description. 5. Select: key stats, compared with the installed
one. 6. Buy & Equip (Equip if already owned). 7. Back to the card with that mount updated.
Back returns to the previous page without losing the selected vehicle or mount.

## In the hangar
The real ship / mech stays visible. The small picture in the panel is the selection interface; the full model shows the
result. On install a maintenance drone carries the part to the mount and attaches it; the model shows the new weapon.
The panel shows "Installing", then "Equipped".

## Abilities
Same process: select a slot, browse compatible modules (power cells, shields, sensors ... compact cards), inspect,
equip. Details open only when selected.

## Calls
Incoming calls appear in the same panel: the caller's animated portrait, dialogue and response choices. When the call
ends the previous page and selection return. Two callers: the panel splits into two compact call views; a second small
adjoining window only when needed.

## Keeping the screen clear
The panel minimises to a small icon. Lists scroll inside it, zoom stays inside it. One main task at a time.
