# Homelancer – room art STYLE (paste at the top of EVERY image and video prompt)

This block is the owner's standing style. It goes, word for word, at the start of every room picture, join fill,
band extension and animation prompt, for every faction. A picture or clip that does not match it is redone, not used.

```
STYLE (always): Epic Seven anime splash-art style — hand-drawn anime / cel-shaded illustration, clean ink line art,
bright vivid colours, soft glowing highlights, painterly detailed backgrounds, the look of Epic Seven's character
splash art and cutscenes. Futuristic sci-fi MMO setting in space (Star Citizen level of detail in the architecture,
ships and gear). NOT photorealistic, NOT 3D-render, NOT dark gritty concept art, NOT semi-realistic faces.
Characters: anime faces and proportions, stylish outfits and armour, expressive poses. Multi-race crews where the
faction calls for it: humans plus alien races (elf-eared, horned, furred beast-folk, blue- or purple-skinned humanoids,
androids), mixed men and women, all in the same Epic Seven anime style.
Lighting: bright and clear, the room well lit by the faction's colours; space or a planet visible through windows.
Animation keeps the SAME style: every frame stays anime splash-art; no switch to realistic motion or textures.
```

Per faction, add after it: the faction's colours, the station and system names, and the room.

Reference for the look: the Unity V2 rooms (art/rooms_v2/unity/) are on style; Liberator's first shipyard /
mech dealer / mission center / private quarters are on style. Too dark / realistic = redo.
