## Combat feedback, radio faces and sound
- Enemy shields: raiders 30, corsairs 50. Shots drain the shield first (blue flash, blue damage number), then the
  hull (orange). Shields recharge after 4 s without hits. Every enemy within 900 m shows a shield bar over a hull bar;
  the target shows bigger bars with numbers, and the waypoint panel shows both.
- Laser bolts: 14 m glowing core + wide glow + a bright head sprite, and a muzzle flash, so they read on phones.
- Missiles: flickering two-layer exhaust flame out of the tail, a glowing trail and a thin smoke trail.
- Radio calls show the caller's face (assets/portraits/<face>_<normal|serious|angry|sad|smile>.png). A line can start
  with `[expression]` to choose the face. Friendlies: Vale, Oduya, Rennick, Amari. Bosses: Shade (Hoard's lieutenant,
  Solara raiders) and Hoard (Vega corsair warlord). Enemy pilots from the enemy dossiers hail you: Scar Jackal and
  Ember Wraith (raiders), Iron Revenant and Frost Banshee (corsairs).
- Sound (autoload `Sfx`, scripts/sfx.gd; WAVs synthesized by tools/sfx/make_sfx.py): radio chirp on open/close,
  Star Fox-style mumble pitched per character while the line is up, lasers, missile launch, shield/hull hits,
  explosions. The VOICE button on the call panel switches to reading lines aloud with the device's text-to-speech
  (falls back to mumble when the device has none).
- `HL_SHOWCASE=1 HL_SHOT_DIR=/path godot --path . -- --autotest` runs a short scripted fight and saves screenshots.
Route test: RESULT 36/36 PASS (desktop and phone-size web). Web pck 12.2 MB.

## Freelancer-style HUD (owner's layout, Sept 30)
- Old side columns (six system cards with AUTO/MAN, and the vertical button stacks) are gone.
- Top left: SHIELD (uses a shield charge, count badge) · REPAIR (repair kit, count badge) · TRACTOR; under them
  STOP · WARP. Top right mirrors it: three weapon slots, then THRUST · KILL (KILL against the right edge).
- Weapon slots (`GS.slots`, `Data.SLOT_ITEMS`): default LIGHT MISSILE · HEAVY MISSILE · MINE. Later the hub will let
  you fit other items. Light missile does 30% of the target's hull (at least 45); heavy does 75 and costs 200 cr
  (Equipment dealer sells both). Cadet 2 heavies, Ranger 3, Lancer 4.
- Lasers fire by themselves when a hostile is in the reticle; energy cells top up on their own. Shield, repair,
  missiles and mines are button-only now.
- Mines sit still until a hostile comes within 170 m, then wake (beep) and chase it.
- Destroyed ships drop 1–2 glowing cargo pods (40% of the kill reward each). TRACTOR pulls every pod within 700 m to
  you for 4 s; flying within 14 m also picks one up. The TRACTOR badge shows how many pods are floating.
- Status: segmented colour bars at the top centre, shield blue, hull green→yellow→red, energy gold, no numbers; they
  flash red below 25%. MAP · VIEW · LOG · CALL · TARGET · GO TO sit under the bars; HANG UP is on the call panel.
- New sounds: tractor, pickup, mine_wake, button (tools/sfx/make_sfx.py).

## Mechs + three-part damage (first pass, owner spec Sept 30)
- Assault Mech enemy (`Data.ENEMIES.mech`, model `assets/mechs/tan_navy_mecha.glb`, ShipFactory key `mech_tan`, 11 units
  tall, about small-ship size). The last ship in a patrol group is a mech half the time. Arm guns fire alternately, and a
  chest cannon (slower, 1.8x damage) keeps firing with no arms left.
- Every unit, including the player ship, has three sections: LEFT (wing/arm) · CORE · RIGHT, each side worth 40% of the
  hull (`Data.SECTION_SHARE`). Shields soak damage first. A laser hit lands on the side under the hit point; missiles, mines and centre hits go to the core.
- A side reaching zero: one short explosion (flash, fireball, smoke puff), the part disappears, its gun stops, and the target
  message says so. After that there is one tiny spark (and sometimes a wisp of smoke) every 3–5 s, only within 1.4 km.
  Core at zero: a big blast, the unit shrinks away in 0.35 s and drops loot.
- How parts vanish, with no runtime mesh cutting: ships are split once per model type into three index sets (cached in
  `scripts/sections.gd`), and a lost wing is just hidden. Mechs are untouched: a lost arm's UpperArm bone is scaled to
  zero, so the arm and anything in its hand collapse into the shoulder.
- Mech flight pose: leans forward 10–22° and swings the arms back 10–35° with speed, leans into turns, and eases back
  to neutral (`Sections.pose_mech`). `tools/shipkit/mech_pose_demo.gd` renders neutral / cruise / boost / arm-lost sheets.
- The player's wings use the same code (`GS.wing_l/r`). A lost wing takes its guns with it; docking repairs it. The HUD hull row is
  now [L][CORE][R], and enemy bars show the same, with L/R ARM or WING labels on the target.
- Effect sprites are pooled (reused), which is ready for big fights.
Route test: RESULT 37/37 PASS (new check: arm/wing off, gun offline, sparks, core kill).
