## Freelancer-style HUD (owner's layout, Sept 30)
- Old side columns (six system cards with AUTO/MAN, and the vertical button stacks) are gone.
- Top left: SHIELD (uses a shield charge, count badge) · REPAIR (repair kit, count badge) · TRACTOR; under them
  STOP · WARP. Top right mirrors it: three weapon slots, then THRUST · KILL (KILL against the right edge).
- Weapon slots (`GS.slots`, `Data.SLOT_ITEMS`): default LIGHT MISSILE · HEAVY MISSILE · MINE. Later the hub will let
  you fit other items. Light missile does 30% of the target's hull (at least 45); heavy does 75 and costs 200 cr
  (Equipment dealer sells both). Cadet 2 heavies, Ranger 3, Lancer 4.
- Lasers fire by themselves when a hostile is in the reticle; energy cells top up on their own. Shield, repair,
  missiles and mines are button-only now.
- Mines sit still until a hostile comes within 170 m, then wake (beep) and chase it.
- Destroyed ships drop 1–2 glowing cargo pods (40% of the kill reward each). TRACTOR pulls every pod within 700 m to
  you for 4 s; flying within 14 m also picks one up. The TRACTOR badge shows how many pods are floating.
- Status: segmented colour bars at the top centre, shield blue, hull green→yellow→red, energy gold, no numbers; they
  flash red below 25%. MAP · VIEW · LOG · CALL · TARGET · GO TO sit under the bars; HANG UP is on the call panel.
- New sounds: tractor, pickup, mine_wake, button (tools/sfx/make_sfx.py).

## Mechs + three-part damage (first pass, owner spec Sept 30)
- Assault Mech enemy (`Data.ENEMIES.mech`, model `assets/mechs/tan_navy_mecha.glb`, ShipFactory key `mech_tan`, 11 units
  tall, about small-ship size). The last ship in a patrol group is a mech half the time. Arm guns fire alternately, and a
  chest cannon (slower, 1.8x damage) keeps firing with no arms left.
- Every unit, including the player ship, has three sections: LEFT (wing/arm) · CORE · RIGHT, each side worth 40% of the
  hull (`Data.SECTION_SHARE`). Shields soak damage first. A laser hit lands on the side under the hit point; missiles, mines and centre hits go to the core.
- A side reaching zero: one short explosion (flash, fireball, smoke puff), the part disappears, its gun stops, and the target
  message says so. After that there is one tiny spark (and sometimes a wisp of smoke) every 3–5 s, only within 1.4 km.
  Core at zero: a big blast, the unit shrinks away in 0.35 s and drops loot.
- How parts vanish, with no runtime mesh cutting: ships are split once per model type into three index sets (cached in
  `scripts/sections.gd`), and a lost wing is just hidden. Mechs are untouched: a lost arm's UpperArm bone is scaled to
  zero, so the arm and anything in its hand collapse into the shoulder.
- Mech flight pose: leans forward 10–22° and swings the arms back 10–35° with speed, leans into turns, and eases back
  to neutral (`Sections.pose_mech`). `tools/shipkit/mech_pose_demo.gd` renders neutral / cruise / boost / arm-lost sheets.
- The player's wings use the same code (`GS.wing_l/r`). A lost wing takes its guns with it; docking repairs it. The HUD hull row is
  now [L][CORE][R], and enemy bars show the same, with L/R ARM or WING labels on the target.
- Effect sprites are pooled (reused), which is ready for big fights.
Route test: RESULT 37/37 PASS (new check: arm/wing off, gun offline, sparks, core kill).

## Planets: atmosphere entry, tiled surfaces with wrap-around, fast travel (prototype, owner spec Sept 30)
How it works (chosen to fit the existing build): a planet surface is the SAME flight scene (`SpaceSystem`) set up in
surface mode (`setup_surface`), so flight, combat, mechs, HUD, docking and hubs all work unchanged. Only one tile is
loaded at a time; `scripts/surface.gd` holds the planet/biome data and builds a tile.
- Tiles: 5 km square playable area. Terrain is a 112x112 height grid from one noise field in planet coordinates, so
  neighbours match; it extends 1.6 km past each edge, and depth fog (clear to 1.1 km, thick by 4.3 km) hides where it
  ends. Biomes: ocean, coast, desert, mountains, city, forest, ice, industrial, wasteland, alien jungle, volcanic
  (sky, fog, water and terrain colours per biome). Towns are one MultiMesh of buildings plus a landing pad (the tile's
  dockable port). Rocky biomes get rock spires. Building a tile takes about 0.18 s on desktop; built terrain is cached.
- Planets: New Terra (Solara) is 3x3 with 5 landing sites (Port Meridian capital, Saltmarsh Docks, Dust Flats Colony,
  Ridgeback Base, Iron Foundry). Eden Prime (Vega) is 2x2 with 2. A moon would be `"grid": 1`.
- Wrap: the grid is a torus. East of the last column is column 0, and north of row 0 is the last row. Crossing any edge plays a
  0.35 s cloud pass with streaks and a whoosh, loads the neighbour, and moves you to the opposite edge with the same heading
  and speed.
- Option A, fly in: planets with a surface are no longer solid. Crossing the atmosphere line plays ENTERING
  ATMOSPHERE (heat glow, clouds, camera shake, rumble), and the tile loads during the white-out. You arrive over the tile
  under where you hit the atmosphere, still on the same heading. Climbing above 2,600 m plays LEAVING ATMOSPHERE and puts you back in space
  above that part of the planet.
- Option B, fast travel: docking at the planet (orbital port) or at any town adds SURFACE TRAVEL to the hub menu,
  a list of every landing site. Pick one and you go straight to its pad (DESCENDING, then the launch sequence).
- Ground: you can't go below the terrain or water; a hard impact costs hull. Enemies are kept above the ground. Each
  tile has a patrol of 2 (sometimes a mech).
- HUD in surface mode: sector name and altitude on the info line, ALT under speed. MAP shows the sector name.
Route test: RESULT 42/42 PASS (new: atmosphere entry, tile edge, world wrap, climb to orbit, hub fast travel).

## Terrain detail + seamless borders + red canyon (owner's canyon concept, Sept 30)
- Borders are seamless by construction (`Surface.sample`). There is one noise field for the planet. Across the wrap line it is
  cross-faded with its copy one planet-width away, so east of the last column meets column 0 exactly. Each biome is pure in
  the middle 60 % of its tile and blends shape and colour into its neighbour over the outer 20 %. The route test measures
  the step at several borders, including the wrap: 0.00 m.
- Detail without heavy meshes (`assets/terrain/terrain.gdshader`): the owner's canyon ground photo is made seamless
  (`tools/terrain/make_ground.py`, which also writes a bump map from its brightness) and tiled in world space over every
  tile, tinted by the biome colour. A second bump map is 'render clouds' noise (NoiseTexture2D, made at load). Steep
  slopes get the texture from the side plus horizontal rock strata; grit fades with distance so it doesn't shimmer.
- Red canyon biome (New Terra sector C3, replaces the wasteland): stepped flat-topped mesas with sheer layered walls and
  rivers winding along the canyon floor. The terrain grid is now 128x128.
- Sector borders are hidden in a weather front that suits where you're going: SANDSTORM (desert/canyon/wasteland), SNOW SQUALL,
  ASH CLOUD, TURBULENCE (mountains), SMOG BANK, or RAIN SQUALL, with a "Weather front ahead" warning 350 m before the edge.
- The owner's Tripo canyon mesh (a single hand-made tile, very high poly, edges don't match anything) is best used later as a hero
  landmark prop (decimated, like the ships), not as a whole tile.
Route test: RESULT 42/42 PASS.
