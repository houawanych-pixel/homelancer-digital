# 360 room art: what exists, what's needed (one world, two rules)

Every station and ship has one 3D deck. Signs on the deck open a place's 360 room and its menu.
A sign without 360 art yet opens its menu over the 3D view until the picture exists.

## Stations: done
All 8 factions (Unity, Orion, Imperium, Liberator, Covenant, Elyza, Solarion, Savagers) have the full set of 10:
main hub, pilots' lounge (BAR), mission center, weapons (EQUIPMENT), shipyard (SHIP DEALER), mech dealer,
medical bay, supplies, command, private quarters. Some named locations (assets/rooms/loc) have their own sets.

## Ships: needed (the big boardable classes)
